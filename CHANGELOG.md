# Changelog

All notable changes to DeckHTML are documented in this file.

The project follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/). Historical details are available in the [commit history](https://github.com/deckflow/deckhtml/commits/main).

## [0.6.1]

### Fixed

- Improve automatic detection of semantic multi-slide containers.
- Clamp negative table coordinates and normalize scrolling to avoid invalid PowerPoint offsets.

## [0.6.0]

### Added

- Inspect, screenshot, or skip iframe content during local conversion.

## [0.5.0]

### Added

- Expand the slide-transition catalog to 55 effects, including PowerPoint 2014/2015 effects and Morph.
- Export PowerPoint slide transitions with named, cyclic, random, or disabled selection.
- Export supported CSS-transition entrance animations.
